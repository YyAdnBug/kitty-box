The BiRefNet project and model are presented by their authors under the MIT
License. The Hugging Face model card displays an MIT badge, although that model
repository does not contain a separate license file. The upstream MIT notice is
reproduced in LICENSE-BIREFNET.

The conversion is adapted from imperatormk/coreml-birefnet, including its
fixed-shape loader, deformable-convolution lowering, and rank-5 Swin rewrite:
https://github.com/imperatormk/coreml-birefnet

The prior-art MIT notice is reproduced in LICENSE-COREML-BIREFNET.

Local modifications pin and verify the upstream source and weights, expose only
the final mask logits, target iOS 17, provide separately validated FP32 and
FP16-compute/FP32-I/O builds, add deterministic verification and packaging,
and document the tested CPU-only runtime policy.
